mimictrace

mimictrace is a master's thesis project in cybersecurity by Pavlo Kulakov. It studies newly registered internet domains in order to detect typosquatting and brand impersonation.

The "mimictrace backup" Google application is used only by the author. It lets the author's own server store encrypted backups of the project's research data in the author's own Google Drive. It is not offered to other users.
